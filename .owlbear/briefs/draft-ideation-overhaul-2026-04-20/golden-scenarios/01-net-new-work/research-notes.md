# Research Notes

## Verified findings

- The current ideation surface stores the canonical decision trail in `decisions.md` and the approved deliverable in `brief.md`.
- No export-oriented prompt or agent exists today.
- The blackboard already separates state, research, and synthesis well enough for a narrow export feature.

## Candidate implications

- A minimal exporter can probably read only `brief.md` and `decisions.md` at first.
- The first release does not need a late-panel replay or a second summary artifact.

## Open research questions

- Should the export surface preserve rejected options or only the final chosen path?
- Does the user want plain markdown only, or a second machine-readable format later?

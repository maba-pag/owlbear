The Brief defines the complete feature — everything listed is a requirement. No "mandatory vs recommended" tiering. The planner handles implementation sequencing (what depends on what), not scoping (what to include). If something shouldn't be built, it doesn't appear in the Brief at all. During O15 triage, when the Critic argues "this isn't necessary for the feature to work," evaluate whether the item is wanted — not whether it's the minimum viable subset. This prevents the pattern where "recommended" items get deprioritized into oblivion, leaving the product at 60% — functional but not worth using. Codified in w-ideation-mediation Step 5 rule 6 and O15 rule 9.

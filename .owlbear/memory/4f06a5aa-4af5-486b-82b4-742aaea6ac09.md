Task #2003 showed that generic workspace search and challenger discovery can omit `.owlbear/kanban/archive/` and falsely report completed dependency IDs as missing. Before repairing a supposedly dangling Kanban edge, call `list_dir` on the exact hidden archive directory, then `read_file` the exact `{id}-*.md` record and cross-check MCP `dep_status`. Treat a search miss as evidence-access failure when those direct checks succeed.

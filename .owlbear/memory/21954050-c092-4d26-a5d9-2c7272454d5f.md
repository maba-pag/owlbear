When reviewing `IngestCoordinator.refresh` in `serve/knowledge/src/owlbear_knowledge/ingest_coordinator.py`, distinguish three branches. Zero documents with fetch errors already continues and leaves `sources_refreshed` at 0. Fetched documents whose processing all fails are absorbed by `_process_document`, yet refresh currently updates freshness and increments the counter; this is the false-success branch. Zero documents with zero errors legitimately increments today, so suppressing the counter on unqualified "zero successes" would break no-op refresh semantics.

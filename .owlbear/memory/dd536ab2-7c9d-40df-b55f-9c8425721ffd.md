When a plan or packet cites `tests/test_persistence_source_wiring.py` as proof that a new store binds to its injected root, reject it as the wrong proof surface: the file contains only `class TestQdrantPersistencePathWiring` asserting `owlbear_knowledge_mcp.server.app_lifespan` wires `QdrantVectorStore` to `_DEFAULT_QDRANT_PATH` (tests/test_persistence_source_wiring.py:11-78). Its workspace-scoped name does not generalise to Delivery, Memory, or Cockpit persistence; a Delivery store's root-derivation and containment must be proved by its own owning test under serve/delivery/tests/.

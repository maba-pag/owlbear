When `DeliveryPlanContext.launch.builder_handoff_context.route` is `same-outcome-planner`, the plan must keep the task named `original_task_id` with `commitment_ids` and `maintained_surfaces` exactly equal, including order, to the handoff lists, and its `dependency_ids` must already be completed. Enforced by `runtime_reads.py::_validate_planner_return_plan`, `runtime_receipts.py::_validate_task_lineage` and `runtime_models.py::_validate_builder_handoff`; renaming, reordering or narrowing it fails. To split a multi-domain task, keep it first and verbatim, restrict its edit authority in constraints to one domain, and give other surfaces (including files outside the fixed list) to successor tasks. Confirm-check `procedure` is capped at 512 chars; measure after inserting full 40-hex commits.

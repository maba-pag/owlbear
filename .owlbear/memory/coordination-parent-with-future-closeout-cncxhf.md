If a coordination-parent task includes an AC like 'auditor closes this parent after child tasks are archived', do not PASS review while children remain active. Treat it as a contract/lifecycle mismatch, reject to backlog for architect re-scope or parking, and cite current child statuses plus the literal AC line.

OUT-005-TASK-002 exposed a replay pitfall in `portfolio_application.py`: a caller-side safety preflight stricter than `ChangeWorkspaceManager.restart` rejected legitimate states left after attempt-ref creation, worktree removal, branch reset, or worktree add. When composing a replayable multi-step primitive, enumerate and test every internal interruption state through the public caller; admit only states carrying the primitive's immutable identity and ancestry proofs, then resume with that preserved identity.

In serve/knowledge/src/owlbear_knowledge/_ssrf.py, check_url_allowed loops over socket.getaddrinfo results and skips families outside AF_INET/AF_INET6. A result containing no IPv4/IPv6 entry therefore falls through and permits acquisition. When reviewing Knowledge SSRF safety or deterministic HTTP proof, require this case to fail closed and require an explicit resolver dependency so the real policy can be exercised without permitting loopback/private addresses.

When adding DeliveryDiscoveryErrorCode values in serve/delivery/src/owlbear_delivery/delivery_contract_discovery.py, preserve their startup semantics in both _ADMISSION_ONLY_ERRORS and delivery_application_loader._RECOVERABLE_ADMISSION_ERRORS. Otherwise a bounded read failure for admission.json can unintentionally become fatal startup failure.

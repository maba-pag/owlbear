In share/agents/*.agent.md, Channel A is the concise inline/summary signal and Channel B is the structured deliverable. Standalone agents with no Delivery integration declare Channel B not applicable (see share/agents/knowledge-enricher.agent.md and share/agents/memory-curator.agent.md). When adding role-specific reporting guidance to a standalone agent, extend Channel A rather than filling Channel B, so the two channels do not silently specify the same report twice.

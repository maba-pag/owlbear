When reviewing `.owlbear/delivery/worktrees/<change_id>/`, do not infer absence from `grep_search` or `file_search` results against root `serve/` and `tests/`; explicit worktree include patterns may still miss ignored worktree source. Use absolute-path `read_file` or `list_dir` for worktree evidence, and treat root-checkout search hits only as integration-target evidence.

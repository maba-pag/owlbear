When writing or converting target-contract decision blocks, quote each decision's recorded source verbatim (the intent.md Decisions bullet, Preserved Behavior, Product Promise or Accepted Exclusions text). Paraphrased statements repeatedly failed designer challenge for dropping clauses. Record designer consequences, refinements and challenger repairs as separate `approved` decisions; keep only the user's own words as `decided`. Link each commitment to every decision it rests on. Verify offline first with `owlbear_delivery.target_contract.compile_delivery_contract`: outcomes, scopes, and commitment class and statement must equal the admitted contract, proving the rewrite invalidates nothing.

Before failing a retry cycle on a stricter AC interpretation, compare the proposed finding against prior review and builder notes in the task body. If the task record already accepted the narrower reading and the AC text did not change, treat the stricter reading as contract expansion or AC ambiguity, not a fresh proof gap.

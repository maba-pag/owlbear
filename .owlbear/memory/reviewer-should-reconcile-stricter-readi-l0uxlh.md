On retry review, do not turn an unchanged ambiguous AC into a stricter fresh proof gap after prior review or builder notes accepted a narrower reading. Treat the stricter reading as contract expansion or AC ambiguity unless the earlier acceptance plainly contradicted the written AC; cite the unchanged AC text and prior task note when routing.

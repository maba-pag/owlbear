In task #2006, `serve/kanban/src/owlbear_kanban/runtime_transaction.py` showed that recover-to-completion after process interruption does not prove an operation's caught write-failure abort contract. When an AC requires both, keep manifests for crashes but have the live public operation explicitly roll back prepared participants after catching an injected failure, and test before publication, after first publication, and before manifest cleanup.

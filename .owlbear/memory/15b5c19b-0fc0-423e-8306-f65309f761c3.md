In `serve/delivery/src/owlbear_delivery/delivery_runtime.py`, `parse_delivery_frontier()` checks JSON shape, schema version, and in-model validators. Change-contract-relative checks remain in `DeliveryRuntime._validate_frontier()`, invoked from `_read()`. Designs that centralize parsing must not claim parser-only consumers gain runtime contract-relative validation or move those checks into the parser unintentionally.

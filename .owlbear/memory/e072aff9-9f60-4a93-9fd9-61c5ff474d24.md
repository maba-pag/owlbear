Adding a scope to `.github/sync-manifest.json` is insufficient: `.github/workflows/sync-to-main.yml` allowlists serve scope names and derives `SYNC_<UPPER>` and `<UPPER>_PATHS` shell variables. A plan adding a hyphenated serve scope must also register its workflow input/env/allowlist and normalize `-` to `_` at every derivation; `tests/test_sync_manifest.py` needs a regression for this production boundary.

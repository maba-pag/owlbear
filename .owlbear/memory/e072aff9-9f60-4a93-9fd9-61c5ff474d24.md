When adding a serve scope to consumer-branch sync, update both `.github/sync-manifest.json` and the corresponding scope registration, allowlist, and identifier derivation in `.github/workflows/sync-to-main.yml`. Keep normalization consistent for hyphenated scope names and add a regression in `tests/test_sync_manifest.py`; a manifest-only change can leave the scope out of generated consumer output.

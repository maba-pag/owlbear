For headed Chromium proof of an unpacked Manifest V3 extension, do not assert service-worker presence after navigation: idle workers may stop before inspection and make the proof flaky. Use a controlled content script that marks the fixture page, then wait for the DOM marker. This directly proves extension execution and is stable across worker lifecycle timing.

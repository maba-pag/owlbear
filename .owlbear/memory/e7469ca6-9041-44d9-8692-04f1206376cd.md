In `serve/memory-mcp`, `recall_memory` recognizes callers through `_recognized_agent_names(engine)`, which derives non-deleted provenance and scope names from the live `MemoryEngine`. When testing identity admission or unknown-agent fallback, seed a real `MemoryEngine` and call the public tool through its lifespan context; a bare mocked engine or mocked validation method can make the assertion vacuous. Use the real-engine setup in `tests/test_memory_agent_identity.py` and verify the implementation in `serve/memory-mcp/src/owlbear_memory_mcp/tools.py`; do not assume a `_agents_from_ctx(ctx).require(...)` API.

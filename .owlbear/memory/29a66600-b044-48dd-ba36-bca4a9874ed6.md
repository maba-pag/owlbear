In `owlbear_delivery`, `engine-action-failed` readiness does not imply the engine owner acts next. A preserved target-sync merge conflict also reports `blocked/engine-action-failed` (operation `resolve-attention`), but its attention card correctly names the user, who runs `/resolve-target-conflict`. Readiness `next_actor` in `_card_readiness` (application_readiness.py) inherits `card.next_actor`. When changing a publication card's owner or adding an `engine-action-*` owner rule, assert ownership for both an interrupted completion (`test_*_contains_unknown_custody_without_repeating_effects[observe-acceptance]`, which needs `agent`) and a target-sync conflict (`test_engine_target_sync_conflict_exit_releases_retained_engine_custody`, which needs `you`). Before PR #438 nothing tested the conflict owner, and a blanket engine-custody→agent rule (#437) regressed live conflicts from `you` to `agent`.

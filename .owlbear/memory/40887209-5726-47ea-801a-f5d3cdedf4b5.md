OwlBear Delivery `DeliveryOutcome.dependency_ids` in `serve/delivery/src/owlbear_delivery/target_contract.py` only references outcomes within one Change; admission does not enforce sibling Change lifecycle dependencies. A design that waits for another Change must name the operator/orchestration sequencing gate and revalidate against the completed target head rather than claiming engine-enforced ordering.

serve/delivery uses two non-interchangeable canonical encodings. target_contract._canonical_json emits json.dumps(..., ensure_ascii=False, sort_keys=True, separators=(',',':')) plus a trailing newline and produces DeliveryContract digests, while target_admission._canonical_json emits json.dumps(..., sort_keys=True, separators=(',',':')) with ensure_ascii defaulting to True and no newline and produces receipt_id and source_bindings_digest. When re-deriving a digest outside those modules, match the encoding of the field being compared; mixing them diverges on non-ASCII content. Prove it by admitting a real Change and asserting the discovery observation has no error.

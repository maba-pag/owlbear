When reviewing a "no false success" claim in owlbear_knowledge.ingest_coordinator.refresh, a non-empty RefreshResult.errors tuple does not prove suppression, because partial refreshes legitimately carry errors while still marking the source refreshed. Require proof that asserts the state mutation itself — sources.update_source.assert_not_called() together with sources_refreshed == 0 for total failure, and assert_called_once() with sources_refreshed == 1 for the partial and zero-document no-op paths. Verified at tests/test_knowledge_failure_contract.py::test_ingest_records_operation_failures_and_refresh_suppresses_false_success and ::test_refresh_preserves_partial_success_and_zero_document_noop.

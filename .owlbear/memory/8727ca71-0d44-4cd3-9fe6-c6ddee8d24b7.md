The runtime assess_memories contract requires each assessment to contain `entry_id` and `bucket`; accepted buckets are `outstanding`, `unremarkable`, `didnt_use`, and `factually_wrong`. The current h-mcp-memory handbook omits this schema, and fields such as `outcome` or buckets like `helpful` and `not_applicable` are rejected.

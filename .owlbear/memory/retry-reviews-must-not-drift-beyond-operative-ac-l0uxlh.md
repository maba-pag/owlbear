In retry or consolidation review, check whether a new proof gap comes from changed evidence or from a stricter reading of the same accepted AC. If the stricter reading was not part of the prior operative contract, record it as clarification or follow-up work; still block when earlier acceptance contradicted the written AC or current evidence shows a real regression.

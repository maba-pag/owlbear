In `serve/delivery`, `locked_roots` creates `.storage.lock` and holds an exclusive descriptor lock until context exit. Treat it as a local coordination boundary, not a provider-consistency fence: before extending a Delivery operation’s lock across workspace or provider calls, keep the local read/write region bounded where semantics allow and rely on explicit expected, published, and observed head checks for remote identity. Inspect `serve/delivery/src/owlbear_delivery/storage_io.py`, `portfolio_application.py`, and `change_publication.py`; this prevents long-held local contention from being mistaken for protection against remote races.

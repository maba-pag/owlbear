# Empty State Illustration Prompts

Use these prompts to generate one placeholder illustration per board column status.

## Shared Art Direction

- Character: Owl-bear mascot appears in every image
- Style: Flat line-art with subtle fills, clean outlines, no photorealism
- Palette: Muted pastel colors, low saturation, soft contrast
- Visual tone: Warm but receding, calm, and non-distracting
- Background: Transparent

## Research

- Target status: research
- Dimensions: 320x240 px (4:3 landscape)
- File format: PNG with transparent background
- Creative direction: Owl-bear using a small telescope and looking at a simple night sky with stars and one tiny constellation; keep forms minimal and readable at small size; mood is curious and exploratory while staying subdued.

Prompt:

```text
Create a 320x240 PNG illustration with transparent background in a flat line-art style with subtle pastel fills. Show an owl-bear mascot gently using a small telescope and observing a simple star field with one tiny constellation. Keep composition uncluttered, outlines clean, and details minimal for readability at small size. Use muted low-saturation colors, soft contrast, and a warm but receding visual tone. Avoid dramatic lighting, strong shadows, neon colors, text, or photorealism.
```

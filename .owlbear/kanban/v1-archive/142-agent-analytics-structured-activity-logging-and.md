Beyond usage tracking (tokens/cost): structured logging of what agents DO. Track: tool invocations, delegation chains, success/failure rates, latency per tool, task completion times.

This is the foundation for the self-improvement pipeline — you can't improve what you can't measure. JSONL-based like usage tracking, with aggregation helpers. Feeds into the future improvement-proposal system.
